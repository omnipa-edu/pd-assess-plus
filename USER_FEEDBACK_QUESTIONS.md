# User Feedback & Improvement Questions

This document contains open-ended questions to gather user feedback and guide application improvements. Please share your thoughts, experiences, and suggestions.

---

## 🎯 Overall Experience

### General Impression
1. **What was your first impression when you landed on the homepage?**
   - What caught your attention first?
   - Did the value proposition make sense immediately?
   - Would you change anything about the initial experience?

2. **How would you describe this application to a colleague in one sentence?**

3. **What problem does this application solve for you personally?**

4. **On a scale of 1-10, how likely are you to recommend this application to a colleague? Why?**

5. **What's the ONE thing you wish this application did that it doesn't currently do?**

## 🔐 Authentication & Onboarding

### Sign-Up Experience
6. **Which sign-up method did you choose (email/password, magic link, or Google)?**
   - What influenced your choice?
   - Would you prefer other authentication options (Apple, Microsoft, etc.)?

7. **How did you feel about the password strength indicator?**
   - Was it helpful or annoying?
   - Did it change your behavior when creating a password?

8. **Did the inline validation help or hinder your sign-up process?**
   - Were the error messages clear?
   - Did you understand what was expected?

9. **What questions or concerns did you have during sign-up that weren't addressed?**

10. **If you used magic link (passwordless) sign-in, how was that experience?**
    - Was it confusing or convenient?
    - Would you use it again?

### Onboarding Journey
11. **After signing up, did you know what to do next?**
    - What would have made the next steps clearer?
    - Did you need a tutorial or guided tour?

12. **How long did it take you to create your first assessment?**
    - Was this faster or slower than expected?
    - What slowed you down?

13. **What would have made your onboarding experience smoother?**
